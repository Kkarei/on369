# ON369

**Your digital space. Your way.**

ON369 is a personal **webtop** — a web-based desktop environment that brings your websites, apps, shortcuts, and digital resources together in one customizable space, accessible through a web browser.

Unlike traditional browser bookmarks, which primarily save links for later access, ON369 provides a visual, desktop-like workspace where users can organize their digital resources using icons, folders, and shortcuts. Rather than simply storing links, it gives users a place to arrange and access the tools they use in their digital lives.

## What ON369 Does

- **Organizes your digital life** — Bring your favorite websites, online tools, and resources together in one place.
- **Creates a personal workspace** — Arrange icons, folders, apps, and shortcuts in a layout that suits your preferences.
- **Provides quick access** — Open frequently used websites and tools directly from your workspace.
- **Brings tools together** — Access useful web applications and productivity tools from a single interface.
- **Puts you in control** — Customize your digital environment to reflect how you work, learn, and browse.

## How ON369 Differs from Bookmarks

| Browser Bookmarks | ON369 |
|---|---|
| Primarily saves website links | Organizes links and resources into a visual workspace |
| Usually arranged in lists, menus, or bookmark folders | Uses a desktop-like interface with icons and folders |
| Helps you remember and revisit websites | Helps you organize and access your digital tools and resources |
| Lives within a browser's bookmarking system | Provides a dedicated workspace accessible through a browser |

## Our Vision

ON369 aims to evolve beyond a traditional webtop into a **browser-based personal computer**. The long-term vision is to let users sign in from any compatible device and access their personal digital environment, including their applications, files, settings, and workspace, without depending on a single physical computer.

## Our Mission

To give people greater control over their digital lives by making their online environments organized, accessible, and personal.
