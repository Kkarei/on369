# ON369

**Your personal webtop.**

## What is ON369?

**Webtop** is a word coined from *desktop*. In this context, it refers to a web-based desktop environment that brings websites and web applications together in one customizable web space, accessible through a web browser.

ON369 allows you to see and access your favourite and everyday websites as icons, just like you would on a computer desktop. Instead of keeping all your useful websites buried in browser bookmarks, you can arrange them visually in your own personal workspace using icons and folders.

ON369 goes beyond being a bookmark manager. It transforms the way you organize, access, and interact with your favourite websites by giving you a personal web-based desktop environment.

## Current Features

- **Personalized Workspace** — Arrange website shortcuts, apps, icons, and folders in a desktop-style interface.
- **Centralized Access** — Bring your frequently used websites and online tools together in one place.
- **Visual Organization** — Organize your digital resources in a way that suits your preferences and workflow.
- **Quick Navigation** — Open your favourite websites and tools directly from your workspace.
- **Web-Based Access** — Use your webtop through a browser without installing a traditional desktop application.

## Advantages of Using ON369

- **Declutter Your Bookmarks** — Reduce the clutter in your browser's bookmark manager by moving your frequently used websites into ON369.
- **Browser Independence** — Access your favourite websites from any browser without being tied to a particular browser's bookmarking system.
- **No More Importing and Exporting** — Avoid the inconvenience of repeatedly importing your bookmarks whenever you switch browsers or devices.
- **Access Anywhere** — With cloud saving and synchronization, access your personal webtop from anywhere, on any compatible device.
- **Consistency Across Devices** — Enjoy the same personalized workspace, including its arrangement and appearance, even when accessing it from your mobile phone.

## Future Vision

The idea behind ON369 is to evolve beyond a customizable webtop into a **browser-based personal computer** — a digital environment that follows users across devices.

The long-term vision is to give users a personal digital space where they can access their websites, applications, files, tools, and settings from any device, without depending on a single physical computer.

## Our Mission

To give people greater control over their digital lives by making their online environments more organized, accessible, and personal.
